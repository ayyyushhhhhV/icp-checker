AI Associate Recruiter: an AI assistant that screens and schedules candidates for high-volume frontline hiring
(store staff, delivery associates, warehouse staff, nurses' aides). It cuts time-to-hire and recruiter workload.
Best current customers: large retail chains, quick-commerce and logistics companies, and hospital chains that hire
hundreds of frontline staff every month. Regions: India and the UAE. Typical buyer: Head of Talent Acquisition or
CHRO; TA managers influence the decision. Not a fit: staffing/recruitment agencies (competitors), companies under
200 employees.
